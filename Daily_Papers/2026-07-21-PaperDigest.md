### 🔥 Apple-π: Benchmarking Thinking with Video Towards Law-Grounded Physical Intelligence (Score: 8.1/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Introduces the first benchmark that evaluates video models as world models through a three-stage law-grounded reasoning protocol (Perception, Formulation, Deduction) with hybrid physics-law and MLLM metrics.

### ✨ TimeLens2: Generalist Video Temporal Grounding with Multimodal LLMs (Score: 5.2/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Proposes a set-valued temporal grounding approach with a temporal Wasserstein reward for dense, matching-free feedback in multimodal LLMs.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: The method is evaluated only on video understanding benchmarks, not on robotic manipulation or embodied AI tasks, limiting its direct applicability to the target research interests.
