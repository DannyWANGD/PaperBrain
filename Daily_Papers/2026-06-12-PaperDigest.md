### ✨ Diffusion Policy: Visuomotor Policy Learning via Action Diffusion (Score: 7.9/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Introduces a diffusion-based visuomotor policy that leverages receding horizon control and a time-series diffusion transformer to model multimodal action distributions for robot manipulation.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: The method is limited to imitation learning from demonstrations and does not incorporate reinforcement learning or online adaptation.
