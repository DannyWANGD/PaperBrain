### ✨ GEM-4D: Geometry-Enhanced Video World Models for Robot Manipulation (Score: 7.1/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Distilling dense 4D correspondence from a pretrained geometry foundation model into a video diffusion model to enforce geometric consistency for manipulation.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: Relies on a pretrained geometry model that may not capture all scene dynamics, and the inverse dynamics module may require embodiment-specific training.
- **🔗 Link**: [Web Link](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22882v3)

### ✨ ESI-Bench: Towards Embodied Spatial Intelligence that Closes the Perception-Action Loop (Score: 6.8/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Introduces a benchmark that evaluates embodied spatial intelligence through active perception-action loops, requiring agents to decide actions to gather evidence, unlike passive observation benchmarks.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: The benchmark is simulation-based and may not capture real-world sensor noise and physical constraints; models tested are MLLMs without real embodiment.

### ✨ CEPO: RLVR Self-Distillation using Contrastive Evidence Policy Optimization (Score: 6.0/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Introduces contrastive evidence from wrong-answer rollouts to sharpen credit assignment in RLVR, with theoretical guarantees of safety and sharpening.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: Only tested on multimodal math reasoning; applicability to embodied AI or robot manipulation remains unexplored.
