## 📝 Papers List
### 🔥 When Does LeJEPA Learn a World Model? (Score: 8.2/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Proves that LeJEPA achieves linear identifiability of world latent variables if and only if the latent distribution is Gaussian, providing the first identifiability guarantee for JEPA-based world models.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: The identifiability guarantee is restricted to worlds with Gaussian latent variables and stationary additive-noise transitions, which may not cover all real-world dynamics.
- **🔗 Link**: [[LeJEPA]]
- **👥 Authors**: David Klindt, Yann LeCun, Randall Balestriero
- **Institutions**: Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, New York University, Brown University

### 🔥 Rethinking Muon Beyond Pretraining: Spectral Failures and High-Pass Remedies for VLA and RLVR (Score: 8.0/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Proposes Pion, a high-pass Newton-Schulz iteration that anchors dominant singular values at 1 while suppressing noisy tail components, with per-head mode for preserving pretrained heterogeneity.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: The high-pass filter strength requires tuning, and the method is only validated on VLA and RLVR tasks, not on broader pretraining scenarios.
- **🔗 Link**: [[Pion]]
- **👥 Authors**: Chongyu Fan, Gaowen Liu, Mingyi Hong, Ramana Rao Kompella, Sijia Liu
- **Institutions**: Michigan State University, Cisco, University of Minnesota, IBM Research
