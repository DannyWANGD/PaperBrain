### ✨ TempoVLA: Learning Speed-Controllable Vision-Language-Action Policies (Score: 7.1/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Introduces speed-controllable VLA via action magnitude conditioning and variable-speed trajectory augmentation that re-times demonstrations by merging/splitting actions.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: The method may require retraining the VLA with speed conditioning; dynamic speed control relies on an external multimodal model, adding complexity.
- **🔗 Link**: [Web Link](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06491v1)
- **👥 Authors**: Dong Jing, Jingchen Nie, Tianqi Zhang, Jiaqi Liu, Huaxiu Yao, Zhiwu Lu, Mingyu Ding

### ✨ GRAIL: Generating Humanoid Loco-Manipulation from 3D Assets and Video Priors (Score: 5.8/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: GRAIL introduces a fully digital pipeline that composes 3D assets with video foundation model priors to generate humanoid loco-manipulation trajectories without physical data collection.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: The paper lacks comparisons to alternative data sources and does not provide ablation studies on the contribution of each component.
