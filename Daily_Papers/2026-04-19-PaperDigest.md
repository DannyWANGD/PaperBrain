### 🔥 HybridVLA: Collaborative Diffusion and Autoregression in a Unified Vision-Language-Action Model (Score: 8/10)
- **💡 Innovation**: Integrates autoregressive next-token prediction and diffusion denoising within a single LLM backbone, using a collaborative training recipe and adaptive ensemble to jointly leverage discrete reasoning and continuous action precision.
- **⚠️ Limitations**: Joint optimization of autoregressive and diffusion objectives likely incurs significant computational overhead and requires careful hyperparameter tuning to prevent gradient interference, with limited details on inference latency.
